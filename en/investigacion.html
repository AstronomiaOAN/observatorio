Research groups

Astronomy, Astrophysics and Cosmology

Founded in 1993. Research areas include solar physics, black holes, cosmology, instrumentation, celestial mechanics and relativity. The institutional profile lists Juan Manuel Tejeiro Sarmiento as coordinator.

Institutional profile ↗

Galactic Astronomy, Gravitation and Cosmology

Founded in 1996. Research includes star clusters, galaxy dynamics, gravitational lensing, cosmological magnetic fields and extended theories of gravity. Published coordinator: Leonardo Castañeda Colorado.

Institutional profile ↗

AGNs, Numerical Analysis and Parallel Simulation

Founded in 2001. Combines extragalactic and optical astronomy with observational techniques, code development and numerical analysis. Published coordinator: Mario Armando Higuera Garzón.

Institutional profile ↗

National Astronomical Observatory Heritage

Develops proposals to conserve and share the OAN's tangible and intangible heritage, connecting astronomy and history. Published coordinator: Mario Armando Higuera Garzón.

GoSA · Our star as a laboratory

The Solar Astrophysics Group studies the Sun and the relationship between its physical processes and Earth's environment. A review of its first ten years, published in 2023, documents its research and education in Colombia.

Research includes solar activity, magnetism, observational techniques and data analysis. The article by Santiago Vargas Domínguez, Benjamín Calvo Mozo, Juan Carlos Martínez Oliveros and Juan Camilo Buitrago Casas introduces this community.

Source: GoSA: ten years of solar research, 2023 ↗.
