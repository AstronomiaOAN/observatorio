Information with traceable sources

This portal draws on institutional OAN and Faculty of Science sources, eSPECTRA, scientific publications, cultural archives and media. Consultation date: September 16, 2026.

We provide editorial summaries and links for further reading. The media archive is a selection that can be expanded. Document dates and publication dates are distinguished where available.

Sources may include template errors, outdated links and historical differences. Admissions figures were not reproduced without context, past events were not presented as current listings, and unrelated text was omitted. Operational details are referred to official channels.

Scope of this proposal

Friends of the Observatory is presented as an initiative in development. Contact links prepare messages in the visitor's email application; they do not automatically submit registrations, bookings or payments.

The content, visual identity and data should undergo institutional review before replacing the official portal. This version does not claim approval or administrative representation.
