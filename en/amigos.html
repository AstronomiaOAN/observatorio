INITIATIVE IN DEVELOPMENT

Friends of the Observatory is a proposed community for people who share a curiosity about the universe and an interest in Colombia's scientific heritage.

A meeting place for citizens, students, alumni, educators and organizations wishing to connect with the OAN and help share knowledge.

This is a community proposal. Its structure, participation arrangements and activities must be defined by the Observatory. This website does not offer memberships, fees, donations or member benefits.

Three ways to build a community

Discover

Join visits, conversations and astronomy activities to share the wonder of learning.

Share

Suggest meetings between students, faculty, alumni and people interested in science.

Care for our heritage

Raise awareness of the historic observatory and help people appreciate its heritage through coordinated initiatives.

Interested in the initiative?

Draft an email to the Observatory to express your interest or suggest an idea. It will open in your email application; you decide whether to send it.
