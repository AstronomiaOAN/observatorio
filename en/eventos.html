Meetings that connect astronomy

Conferences, schools and collaborative spaces: a record of meetings supported by the Observatory and its partnerships.

Each event identifies OAN’s documented role: organizer, host, committee member or related activity. LARIM 2026 is organized by Mexican institutions; OAN participates on the scientific committee of its satellite workshop 4WAI. Past dates are retained as an archive, not as open registrations.
