THE OBSERVATORY’S ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

isleIA

More than twenty years of science, connected through artificial intelligence.

An agent for exploring knowledge

isleIA is the first artificial intelligence agent developed by Universidad Nacional de Colombia (UNAL), created at its National Astronomical Observatory (OAN).

Driven by faculty and undergraduate, master’s and doctoral students, the project was officially presented in May 2026. Its main goal is to digitize and connect more than twenty years of the Observatory’s scientific output, theses and historical records.

Report: Televisión UNAL ↗

What can isleIA help with?

01

Fast literature reviews

Helps researchers cross-reference complex information and find scientific background or methods from specific theses, including solar astrophysics, in seconds.

02

Mathematical understanding

Interprets direct questions about formulas and mathematical expressions contained in documents.

03

Research trends

Helps identify how astronomical research methods have evolved over recent decades.

04

Administrative support

Supports the organization and consultation of documents in the institution’s historical archive, alongside its academic uses.
