Instruments & equipment

From collecting light to exploring data: discover the laboratories and access the equipment platform.

Equipment catalogue

Browse photographs, descriptions, technical characteristics and availability in the equipment platform. Consult the live record for each instrument; this page does not reproduce a static inventory.

02

Loans and returns

The platform supports QR identification, activity details, expected return dates, loans involving several items, and return condition reports. Follow its current instructions and contact the laboratory coordinator for access requirements.

03

Observing domes

The large and small domes support observational practice, instrument operation, teaching and research. Explore the laboratory pages for institutional service information.

04

Photography and spectroscopy

The darkroom connects photography and development with introductory spectroscopy and outreach activities.
