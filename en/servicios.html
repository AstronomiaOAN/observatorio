Planetarium Dome

An astronomy projection experience to discover the sky together.

The Planetarium Dome is part of the Observatory's outreach offering. To arrange an educational activity, provide participants' ages, group size, preferred dates and proposed location.

The services team will confirm content, duration, capacity, setup requirements, accessibility and costs. These details are not specified on the institutional page consulted.

Before your visit

Can I visit without a booking?

Request a visit and wait for confirmation. Access to the historic observatory requires advance coordination.

Does the visit include telescope observing?

Check the activity's specific content. Observing depends on the schedule, equipment and sky conditions.

Where can I check fees and group limits?

Use the institutional services form or email serviciosoan_fcbog@unal.edu.co. We do not publish prices here that may become outdated.

How can I check accessibility arrangements?

Tell the services team about your needs before confirming a visit, so they can advise on the route and facilities.
